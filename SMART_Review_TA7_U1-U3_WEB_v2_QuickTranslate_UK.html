📘 Unit 1 – Hobbies

Điểm cần nhớCấu trúcVí dụ
Like / love / enjoy / hate / not likeS + verb + V-ingI enjoy reading books.
Present SimpleS + V/V-s(es)She collects stamps.
Phủ địnhS + do/does not + VHe doesn't play judo.
Câu hỏiDo/Does + S + V...?Does she go camping?
To beam/is/areMy hobby is painting.
Tần suấtalways, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, neverI often read books.

Lỗi hay gặp: Sau do/does dùng động từ nguyên thể; he/she/it cần -s/-es trong câu khẳng định; không dùng am/is/are với động từ thường.

📗 Unit 2 – Healthy Living

Thành phầnVai tròVí dụ
SSubject – chủ ngữMai exercises every day.
VVerb – động từMai exercises every day.
OObject – tân ngữShe drinks water.
AdvAdverbial – trạng ngữShe exercises every morning.

Advice: should + V; shouldn't + V. → You should get enough sleep. / You shouldn't eat too much fast food.

📕 Unit 3 – Community Service

Điểm cần nhớCấu trúcVí dụ
Khẳng địnhS + V-ed / irregular V2We donated books. / We gave food.
Phủ địnhS + didn't + VWe didn't donate money.
Câu hỏiDid + S + V...?Did you join the project?
To bewas / wereShe was proud. They were helpful.
Time expressionsyesterday, last week, ... agoWe helped them last Sunday.

Nhớ: Sau did/didn't dùng V nguyên thể. Không dùng V-ed sau didn't.

📖 Reading 1 – Hobbies

Read the text and choose the best answer.

Mai has several hobbies, but reading books is her favourite. She usually reads in the evening after she finishes her homework. At weekends, she sometimes goes camping with her family. Her brother likes collecting coins, while her sister enjoys drawing. Mai thinks hobbies are useful because they help people relax, learn new skills and spend time with others.

📖 Reading 2 – Community Service

Last Saturday, Nam and his classmates joined a community-service project. In the morning, they cleaned a playground and picked up rubbish. Then they planted flowers near the library. In the afternoon, some students gave old books to children in a rural area. Nam was tired at the end of the day, but he was proud because the project was useful. He wants to join another project next month.

✍️ Writing – Unit 1

Topic: Write 70–100 words about your favourite hobby.

Guiding questions: What is your hobby? When did you start it? Who do you share it with? What do you need? How often/where do you do it? Why do you like it? What benefits does it bring?

Sentence frame: My favourite hobby is ... I started it ... I usually ... I need ... I like it because ... It helps me ...
👀 Show model paragraph

My favourite hobby is reading books. I started this hobby when I was ten years old. I usually read in the evening after I finish my homework. I often read at home, especially at weekends. I need some books and a quiet place. I like reading because it helps me relax and learn new things. It also improves my vocabulary and gives me useful ideas.

✍️ Writing – Unit 2

Topic: Write 70–100 words about how to have a healthy lifestyle.

Ideas: healthy food · exercise · sleep · water · hygiene · bad habits to avoid · benefits.

Sentence frame: To stay healthy, we should ... We should also ... We shouldn't ... It is important to ...
👀 Show model paragraph

To have a healthy lifestyle, we should eat more fruit and vegetables and drink enough water every day. We should exercise regularly and get enough sleep at night. We should also keep our surroundings clean and wash our hands before meals. We shouldn't eat too much fast food or spend too much time on unhealthy habits. These simple things can help us stay fit, active and happy.

✍️ Writing – Unit 3

Topic: Write about a community-service activity you joined.

Guiding questions: When and where? Who joined? What did you do? Who did you help? How did you feel? What did you learn?

Sentence frame: Last ... I joined ... We ... Then we ... I felt ... because ... I learned that ...
👀 Show model paragraph

Last Sunday, I joined a community-service project with my classmates. We cleaned the playground near our school and picked up rubbish. Then we planted some flowers and watered them. We also collected old books for children in a rural area. I felt tired but very happy because our work was useful. I learned that small actions can make our community a better place.

🗣️ Speaking – Unit 1: My Hobby

Speak for 1–2 minutes.

  • What is your hobby?
  • When did you start it?
  • What do you need?
  • How often and where do you do it?
  • Why do you like it?
  • What benefits does it bring?
Speaking frame: My hobby is ... I started ... I usually ... I need ... I enjoy it because ... It helps me ...
👀 Show model speaking

My hobby is gardening. I started it two years ago with my mother. We usually work in our garden at weekends. We grow flowers and some vegetables. I need some seeds, tools and water. I enjoy gardening because it is relaxing and useful. It also helps me learn how plants grow. I feel happy when I see new flowers in our garden.

🗣️ Speaking – Unit 2: Healthy Habits

Key words: healthy food · exercise · sleep · water · hygiene · avoid fast food

Talk about what you do to stay healthy and give at least three pieces of advice.

👀 Show model speaking

To stay healthy, I eat fruit and vegetables and drink enough water. I exercise several times a week and try to get enough sleep. I also wash my hands regularly and keep my room clean. I think we should avoid too much fast food and spend more time doing outdoor activities. These habits help me feel active and comfortable.

🗣️ Speaking – Unit 3: Community Service

Key words: volunteer · clean · pick up rubbish · plant flowers · donate books · help people

Talk about a community-service activity you joined. Use the Past Simple.

👀 Show model speaking

Last month, I joined a community-service activity at a local playground. My friends and I cleaned the area and picked up rubbish. We also planted flowers and gave some old books to children. I was tired after the activity, but I felt proud because we helped other people. I learned that volunteering is useful and meaningful.
